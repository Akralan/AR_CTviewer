The body in mixed reality, driven with the controllers.

Controllers
Triggeron the floor: place the body · on an organ: pull it out · short press on a pulled-out organ: it flies back · on the plane, next to the body: hold and aim where to cut
Joystickleft / right: turn the plane · up / down: tilt it · click: scale 1 ↔ 0.3
Gripon the body: move and turn it
A / Xplane shown → hidden (the cut stays) → off (whole body)
B / Yput everything back · long press: reset everything
